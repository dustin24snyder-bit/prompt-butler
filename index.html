Questions?

Is it really free?
Yes — Prompt Butler runs entirely in your browser. No login and no API key required. An optional Turbo mode can use your own Anthropic key for AI-rewritten prompts, but everything works beautifully without it.
Does it work with ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini?
Yes. Every prompt is portable to any AI, and you can open it directly in ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini in one click — using your existing logged-in account.
What does “skill-aware” mean?
Rocco maps your request against 179 built-in skills and weaves the most relevant ones into the prompt — so you get the depth of an expert, not a generic template.
Can it make image & video prompts?
Absolutely. Image mode produces tailored prompts for Midjourney, DALL·E, Stable Diffusion, Google Imagen, and Sora/GIF — complete with negative prompts and recommended settings.
Where is my data stored?
Only in your browser (localStorage). Nothing is uploaded. Your saved prompts and preferences never leave your device.
